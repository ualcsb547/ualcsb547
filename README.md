## 🚀 Featured Quantitative Projects

### 📈 [quant-finance-stochastic-simulation](https://github.com)
*Advanced stochastic process simulation models written in MATLAB and Python for risk assessment and ruin theory.*
*   **Stochastic Diffusion Models:** Implementation of **Arithmetic and Geometric Brownian Motion (GBM)** solvers leveraging Itô's Lemma to simulate asset trajectories and corporate capital paths over time:
    $$dS_t = \mu S_t dt + \sigma S_t dW_t$$
*   **Quantitative Risk Management:** Large-scale **Monte Carlo Simulations** ($10^4$ to $10^7$ trajectories) to evaluate empirical confidence intervals (Value at Risk - VaR), ruin probabilities, and discrete solvency barrier constraints aligned with Solvency II regulatory standards.
*   **Numerical Integration:** Development of custom pure and grid-based Monte Carlo numerical integrators, alongside compound midpoint rules and geometric methods over triangular meshes for multi-dimensional functions.

### 📐 [numerical-methods-pde-pricing](https://github.com)

*   **ODE Solvers:** Implementation of advanced multi-step numerical methods (Runge-Kutta schemes, Finite Differences) for boundary value problems.
*   **Optimization Engine:** Programmed linear, convex, and gradient-based multivariate optimization algorithms designed to solve resource allocation problems under complex boundary constraints.

### 🧠 [latent-space-geometry-gans](https://github.com)
*My BSc Thesis Project (Grade: 10/10) exploring the intersection of deep learning and matrix geometry.*
